# FeynnLab_Project-2
## Market Segmentation on Electric Vehicles Market

1. **Project Overview**:
   
  This report provides a comprehensive market segmentation analysis of the electric vehicle (EV) 
  market in India, focusing on state-wise sales and vehicle categories, including 2-wheelers and 
  4-wheelers. The goal is to identify key geographic markets and vehicle segments to assist in 
  developing an effective market entry strategy.

  The objective of this analysis is to: 
  • Identify state-level EV sales trends across India. 
  • Understand the penetration rate of electric vehicles in each state. 
  • Segment states into clusters based on EV sales performance and penetration. 
  • Analyse vehicle category distribution within each cluster. 
  
  The findings will support strategic decision-making, such as where to focus marketing efforts 
  and expand infrastructure for EV adoption.

  
2. **Key Features**
   
    • State-wise Sales Data: The number of electric vehicles sold per state for different 
    categories (2-wheelers and 4-wheelers).
   
    • Time Series: Quarterly sales data from 2021 to 2024.
   
    • Penetration Rate: Calculated as the percentage of electric vehicles sold out of total 
    vehicles sold in each state.
   
    • Categorization: States have been categorized into five regions: North, South, East, 
    West, Central and North-East.

   
3. **Technologies Used**

    Clustering Algorithms:
      K-Means: For customer or region segmentation.
    PCA: For reducing data dimensionality while retaining meaningful features.

   
4. **Datasets**
   
   The Datasets were used for this analysis: 
    1. Electric Vehicle Sales by State: Contains state-wise EV sales data, including vehicle 
    categories and total vehicles sold. 
    2. Electric Vehicle Sales by Makers: Details the sales data from different EV 
    manufacturers. 
    3. Date Data: Provides temporal data to map sales across specific time periods. 
    4. State category: Contains states and their corresponding regions: West, North, East, 
    South, Central, and North-East. It is used for regional segmentation in market analysis.
